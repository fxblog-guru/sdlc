# Prioritisation (G6)

Read this when a plan has more tasks than one release takes, when `ideas.md` holds more than a few
open rows, or when someone asks "what first?". The G6 checklist row 25 cites this file. A junior
plan lists everything at once. A principal plan says what ships first, what is cut, and why that
order buys the most value or removes the most risk soonest.

## Principles

| # | Principle | Why | When it does not hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Order by value over effort, adjusted for confidence | A big idea nobody is sure of is worth less than a sure small one (RICE, ICE) | Compliance deadlines: they order themselves |
| 2 | Risk first | The task most likely to change the design is done before the tasks that depend on the design | — |
| 3 | A walking skeleton before depth | One thin path through every layer proves the plan; then widen | A change inside one layer |
| 4 | Must, should, could, won't — and "won't" is written | An unwritten cut is renegotiated every week (MoSCoW) | — |
| 5 | Cost of delay is a number | What a week later costs decides between two equal-value items | — |
| 6 | Only a human ranks | The plan proposes an order with reasons; the owner decides (§20) | — |
| 7 | Re-rank when evidence arrives | A ranked list older than its evidence is a guess | — |
| 8 | Fewer things in progress | Finishing one beats starting three; work in progress is cost with no value | — |

## Trade-offs

| Choice | Choose the first when | Choose the second when |
|---|---|---|
| RICE · ICE | Reach can be counted | Early ideas with no reach data |
| Risk first · value first | The design is uncertain | The design is known and value is urgent |
| Vertical slice · layer by layer | Almost always | A platform task every slice needs first |

## Anti-patterns

| What a junior does | How the reviewer spots it |
|---|---|
| Everything is P1 | No task or idea is cut or deferred; no "won't" list |
| Easiest first | The riskiest task (the new integration, the migration) is last |
| An order with no reason | Tasks ranked with no value, effort, confidence or risk stated |
| The agent ranks | A ranked list with no human named as the one who ranked it |

G6 fails a plan that commits one of these.
